Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

This policy covers the private OAuth client “rclone-mtr”, used with the open-source tool rclone. “The client” means that OAuth client; “the operator” means the individual who owns it.

  • The client is for the operator's personal use only. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.
  • Google account data is accessed only for the account that explicitly authorizes the client, and only to read and write files in that account's own Google Drive.
  • All file access happens locally, between the operator's own computer and Google's servers. There is no intermediate server, backend, or hosted component.
  • No data is collected, stored, or shared with anyone. There are no third-party recipients, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising partners.
  • Credentials are stored locally on a single machine and are never shared. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the client's access.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address shown on the app's consent screen.
